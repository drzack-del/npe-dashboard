How it works

Every new patient gets a plan, not a sticky note.

Most patients who don't start right away aren't saying no. They're waiting on something. CadenceIQ tracks what that is and makes sure someone follows up at the right time.

1

Log the exam

After the consult, add the new patient and note the obstacle that's holding them back.

2

Get a custom cadence

That obstacle sets the number of touches the TC owes the patient, and when each one is due.

3

Work today's list

TCs open one list of who to call today, log the outcome, and the next touch is scheduled for them.

A cadence for every obstacle

Different reasons need different follow-up.

A family comparing prices needs a quick call back. A family waiting on Medicaid needs patience, then persistence. Each obstacle has its own required touches, so the TC always knows how many calls a patient is owed and exactly when each one is due.

Accountability

What gets monitored gets done.

In an orthodontic practice, follow-up that nobody checks quietly stops happening. CadenceIQ checks it. Every touch is logged and compared against the cadence, so follow-up isn't something you hope happened. You can see it, and hold the team to it.

Think of it as Lead Sigma for the path from post-consult to start: one defined process, every step measured, and misses visible the same day, not at the end of the month.

  • Every touch logged with the outcome and notes, on one patient history.
  • On-time follow-up rate by TC, so you know who is keeping up.
  • Overdue calls stay visible until they're done. Nobody falls through the cracks.

A metric tracker, like Gaidge

Like Gaidge, CadenceIQ is a metric tracker. It takes your practice data and turns it into the KPIs that matter most, shown in a way that is easy to digest and easy to understand. Conversion, case acceptance, starts, follow-up and TC performance are all laid out so the doctor and the whole team can read them at a glance.
